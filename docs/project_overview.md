# Project Overview – EduNexus

## Problem Statement

Digital education tools are fragmented and often lack adaptivity. Teachers frequently
juggle multiple platforms (e.g. Google Classroom, Quizizz, ChatGPT) for lesson planning,
student management, and assessment, with little integration or personalization.

Students experience one-size-fits-all content, limited interactive feedback, and low
engagement, which reduces the effectiveness of online learning.

## Aim

The aim of this project is to design and implement **EduNexus**, an AI-powered educational
platform that unifies intelligent content generation, adaptive learning, and interactive
classrooms into a cohesive digital ecosystem for teachers and students.

EduNexus should:

- Provide a classroom structure similar to Google Classroom.
- Allow teachers to generate lessons, visuals, and quizzes from natural language prompts.
- Allow teachers to iteratively refine AI-generated lessons by adding, removing, and rewriting sections.
- Allow each classroom to store a syllabus or curriculum brief that guides future lesson generation.
- Give teachers direct authoring and editing control over concepts, lessons, and quizzes alongside AI-assisted generation.
- Adapt quiz difficulty and lesson complexity to each student's ability using concept-graph-level adaptation.
- Use gamification (XP, badges, leaderboards) to drive engagement.
- Offer real-time analytics and accessibility support.

## Research Questions

- How can AI-driven adaptive systems enhance student engagement and learning outcomes?
- What are the limitations of current digital classroom tools in delivering personalized education?
- How can gamification improve motivation and retention in online learning?
- What design principles support accessibility and inclusivity in AI-based education platforms?

## Objectives (Short Version)

1. Conduct a literature review on AI-assisted education, adaptive learning, and gamification.
2. Investigate user needs and design requirements through interviews and questionnaires.
3. Design and develop EduNexus as a web-based platform with:
   - AI lesson generation
   - Teacher-controlled iterative refinement of AI-generated lesson drafts
   - Classroom syllabus-aware generation context
   - Adaptive quizzes
   - Classroom management
   - Gamification and analytics
4. Evaluate the system through user testing and feedback, and propose recommendations for future work.
